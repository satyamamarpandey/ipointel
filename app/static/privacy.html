What we store

Early-access signup stores the information you submit: email, optional name, investor type, market preference, referral information, consent status and timestamps.

How it is used

It is used to operate the waitlist and send product and IPO updates when email delivery is enabled.

The application does not sell waitlist data. Production operators should configure their own retention, data-access and deletion process and have this notice reviewed for the jurisdictions where the service is offered.

Research data

Market research data is sourced from public filings, exchanges and configured secondary providers. Operational logs may record technical request metadata for security and reliability.

Questions about this notice can go to the operator of this deployment. To change what you receive, use the preferences link in any email.
