01THE PROBLEM

One IPO. Three answers.

Most trackers stop at "is it open." We separate the three questions an investor actually has to answer.

01

Invest?

Fundamental quality, valuation and risk — before demand or hype enter the picture.

02

Listing gains?

Demand, pricing and float versus current market conditions — a separate, shorter-horizon signal.

03

Long term?

Business quality, cash flow, governance and whether the price already assumes too much.

One rule: No evidence, no recommendation.
