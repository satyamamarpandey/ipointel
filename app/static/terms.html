Terms of use

IPO Intelligence is research and decision-support software. Scores, probabilities, valuation labels and generated explanations are not guarantees and are not personalized investment advice. IPOs can lose substantial value and allocations, liquidity, taxes and execution differ by investor.

The system may show delayed, incomplete or unavailable data when a source is unavailable. Its reliability gate is designed to suppress actionable recommendations when evidence is insufficient, but users should still verify material facts in the relevant prospectus and exchange/regulator source before acting.

Before a commercial public launch, have these terms and the product's regulatory status reviewed by qualified counsel in each target jurisdiction.
